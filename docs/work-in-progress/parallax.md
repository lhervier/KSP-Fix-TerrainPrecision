## Its terrain and its scatter

**In progress — a craft resting on a rock of Parallax, loaded again and again, without this mod and
with it.** Parallax has bugs of its own in the way: its rocks turn at every load, and near the south pole
a collider leaves its object, both corrected by a change on our fork; this mod makes its scatter grow the
same at every load.
