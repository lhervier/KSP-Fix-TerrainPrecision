## Its terrain and its scatter

**In progress — a craft resting on a rock of Parallax, loaded again and again, without this mod and
with it.** Parallax had bugs of its own in the way: its rocks turned at every load, and near the south
pole a collider left its object, both fixed in Parallax Continued 1.0.5 by a change of ours; this mod
makes its scatter grow the same at every load.
