# Checking the culprit: in flight

Part of [Terrain Precision Fix](../README.md): the measurements that check two culprits in flight, on
stock and with this mod, one page each: [the ground](checking-the-culprit-flight/the-ground.md), where
the quads of the highest level are built and dropped all the time as the craft goes, on Kerbin and on
Earth in Real Solar System, and [the scatter](checking-the-culprit-flight/the-scatter.md), along a
flight over the Mun. The statics are not measured in flight.

## The ground

A rocket launched from the launchpad and left to fly until it falls into the sea, its quads of the
highest level written about once a second. On stock, two quads built at different moments step by up to
22.5 mm where they meet on Kerbin and 289 mm on Earth; with this mod, by 0.67 mm and 2.1 mm at most.

**→ Full page: [Checking the ground: in flight](checking-the-culprit-flight/the-ground.md)**

## The scatter

A pod in orbit 5 km over the Mun, read every two minutes until it crashes into the relief. With the
terrain fix alone, no holder of the scatter is drawn on its quad, by up to 26.5 mm; with the scatter fix
as well, every holder is drawn exactly on its quad, along the whole flight.

**→ Full page: [Checking the scatter: in flight](checking-the-culprit-flight/the-scatter.md)**
